About MRK Global

A focused timber trading partner, built on strong foundations.

MRK Global is an international timber trading company and a wholly owned subsidiary of Rishi Shipping, the established shipping and cargo-handling group based in the Mundra–Kandla region of Gujarat, India.

With a clear focus on timber, we work across sourcing, procurement and international supply. Our approach is grounded in long-term supplier relationships, product understanding and a practical appreciation of the logistics that move commodities across markets.
